---
title: 'Design systems that stay out of the way'
description: 'Tokens, density, and when to stop abstracting components that only exist once.'
pubDate: 2026-06-28
tags: ['design', 'product']
---

A design system earns its keep when it removes decisions, not when it adds wrappers. Start with color, type, spacing, and a handful of primitives.

Skip the mega Button API until three surfaces share the same rules. Document the why in the token name, not a wiki nobody reads.
